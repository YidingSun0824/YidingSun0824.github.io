Course Overview

This page collects selected teaching resources for AMS 310: Probability and Statistics. AMS 310 is a survey course covering data analysis, probability theory, and statistics. Topics include stem-and-leaf displays, box plots, schematic plots, fitting straight-line relationships, discrete and continuous probability distributions, conditional distributions, the binomial distribution, normal and t distributions, confidence intervals, and significance tests.

These materials are intended to help students review core concepts, organize problem-solving methods, and prepare for and exams.

For any questions or suggestions, please contact me or the instructor.
